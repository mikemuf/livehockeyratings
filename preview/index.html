Adjusted scoring

Points against a top-five team count for more than points against a team ranked fiftieth. Every game is weighted by the opponent's rating, so players on hard schedules get proper credit.

Full game logs

Each player's line for every game: ice time, goals, assists, power-play points, plus/minus, with the opponent's rating and rank beside it.

Involvement

The share of his team's goals a player had a hand in, and how he compares with his own teammates. It separates the players who drive a team from the ones riding along.

Power play and ice time
